Privacy Policy for ChronoLens

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Studio Linicro
Zero Data Collected — On-Device Processing

Our Core Privacy Promise

ChronoLens was built with a fundamental commitment to privacy. We believe your home, rooms, family photos, and personal voice memories belong strictly to you. ChronoLens does not require an account, does not connect to external tracking servers, and does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

1. On-Device Processing

  • Camera & Spatial AR Data: Your live camera feed, ARKit tracking data, and room geometry are processed in real time on your iPhone. No video streams or 3D room scans are uploaded to us.
  • Photo Library Access: When you use the on-device Past Moments matcher, Apple's Vision framework runs locally on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers. ChronoLens may also use location metadata already attached to photos in your Camera Roll, only on the device, to help find older photos taken in the same place.
  • Microphone & Voice Time Capsules: When you record an audio note for an AR Time Capsule, the audio file is saved inside the app's local sandbox on your iPhone and is not transmitted to us.

2. Zero Tracking, Zero Analytics, Zero Ads

ChronoLens contains zero third-party SDKs, zero advertising networks, zero telemetry or analytics trackers, and zero cross-app tracking. We do not collect usage diagnostics or behavioral profiles.

3. Storage, Exports & Ownership

All anchored photos, AR Time Capsules, and files you create stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share them. If you export a video or a 3D .usdz file and share it (for example through the iOS share sheet), that file leaves your device only because you chose to share it. You retain ownership and control over everything you capture and export. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data from your device.
